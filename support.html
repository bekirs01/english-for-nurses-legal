Support — English for Nurses: Words

English for Nurses is a premium, paper-styled English course for nurses and nursing students. Lessons stay on the device. This page describes what the current app actually includes.

This is an educational English-learning tool. It is not medical advice and it is not a clinical reference.

Application description

The Home screen offers six destinations: Vocabulary, Phrases, Dialogues, Quiz, Wrong Answers, and Apple Watch. Meanings and interface language can be English or Turkish. English is the language being taught.

Vocabulary learning

About 1,000 nursing words are grouped into 20 categories, including anatomy, vital signs, symptoms, medications, infection control, emergency care, and documentation. Open a category, study the list, mark favorites, mark items as learned, and play pronunciation.

Nursing phrases

About 500 bedside phrases sit in 10 categories, including admission, consent, pain, vital signs, medication talk, procedures, emergency response, reassurance, discharge, and handover.

Flashcards

From a word or phrase set you can open flashcards, flip a card to see the meaning, shuffle, and mark favorite or learned. Cards use the same local progress as the lists.

Nursing dialogues

Twenty-five short clinical dialogues present nurse and patient (or colleague) turns. You can read the scene, follow each turn, and hear on-device English speech. Dialogues are study scripts, not live conversation practice with a person.

Quiz and mistake review

Quiz can mix multiple choice, reverse multiple choice, build-the- answer, and type-the-answer. Missed items collect in Wrong Answers so you can practice them again. A later correct answer can clear that miss.

Offline functionality

Words, phrases, dialogues, images, and interface sounds are bundled in the app. After installation, study does not need an account or a lesson server. Pronunciation uses voices already on the device.

Apple Watch support and limitations

  • Choose a short list of words or phrases on iPhone, then send it to Apple Watch.
  • The Watch stores that list locally so cards can appear when the iPhone is not nearby.
  • The Watch shows one item at a time. It does not include the full iPhone course, flashcards, dialogues, or quiz.
  • The Watch does not play pronunciation audio.
  • A study-window setting exists on iPhone. This version does not send timed operating-system notifications to the Watch.
  • A paired iPhone with the Watch app installed is required to load content.
  • The Watch icon label is the short name “Nurses”.

How local learning progress is stored

Favorites, learned marks, wrong answers, language, sound, haptics, and the Watch list stay in on-device storage. Uninstalling the app removes that data on the device, unless the operating system later restores an older backup. There is no cloud classroom account.

Details are in the Privacy Policy.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-in.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes, for the bundled lessons. You need a network only to download the app from the store, and you may need system voices already installed for speech.

Can I study in Turkish?

Settings can show meanings and chrome in Turkish. The target language remains English.

Is there an alphabet course?

No. This nursing product does not offer Alphabet on Home.

Why is Watch silent?

The companion is a glanceable card list. Speech stays on iPhone.

Will the Watch remind me at a set time?

Not in this version. The study window is a preference stored with your Watch list, not an operating-system alert.

Troubleshooting

  • No sound. Check Settings inside the app, then the device silent switch and volume. Confirm an English voice is installed in system accessibility or speech settings.
  • Watch is empty. On iPhone open Apple Watch, select items, and send the list. Confirm the Watch app is installed and the Watch is paired.
  • Progress disappeared. Progress is local. A new install, a different device, or deleting the app will not carry it unless a system backup restores it.
  • Wrong language on cards. Open Settings and set Translation language to English or Turkish.

How to contact support

Open a GitHub Issue on the public legal repository. A GitHub account is required. Describe the device, operating-system version, and what you expected to happen. Do not send patient information or a child’s personal details.
