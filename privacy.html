Privacy Policy — English for Nurses

Last updated: 12 October 2026
Product: English for Nurses: Words
On-device name: English for Nurses
iOS bundle ID: com.lingocore.englishForNurses
Android application ID: com.lingocore.english_for_nurses
watchOS bundle ID: com.lingocore.englishForNurses.watchkitapp

1. Introduction

This policy describes how English for Nurses handles information in the current iPhone, iPad, Android, and Apple Watch companion design. The app is an educational English-learning tool for nurses and nursing students. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or clinical decision support.

The app is designed to work offline. It does not require an account and does not include a developer-operated cloud service for lessons, progress, or login.

2. Information the application handles

A source-code review of the current build found no user accounts, no authentication, no developer backend, no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting service that transmits data to the developer, and no remote lesson API.

The app may keep the following on the device you use:

  • Identifiers of words and phrases you favorite or mark as learned
  • Quiz miss history used by Wrong Answers
  • Translation-language, sound, and haptics preferences
  • The Apple Watch learning list and study-window settings you choose on iPhone

These values are content IDs and settings. They are not a name, email address, phone number, payment card, precise location, or health record collected by the developer.

3. Local storage and learning progress

Learning progress is stored locally with Flutter shared_preferences, which uses the operating system’s ordinary app storage (UserDefaults on Apple platforms). Vocabulary, phrases, and dialogues ship inside the app bundle. The developer does not receive a copy of your progress.

4. Favorites, quiz history, and preferences

The same on-device store may hold:

  • Favorite word and phrase IDs
  • Learned word and phrase IDs
  • Wrong-answer records (content ID, kind, miss count, last quiz type, timestamp)
  • Selected translation language (currently English or Turkish in Settings)
  • Sound-effect and haptics switches

You can change favorites, learned marks, sound, and haptics in the app. Wrong Answers updates as you practice. There is no developer-side account page for this data.

5. Text-to-speech and audio

Pronunciation uses on-device speech synthesis through flutter_tts. The current product asks the system for an English (United States) voice. The app does not record the microphone and does not operate a network text-to-speech service.

Short interface sounds (tap, correct, incorrect, success) play from files bundled with the app through audioplayers. They are not patient recordings.

Apple, Google, or another voice installed in system Settings may process speech according to that engine’s own behavior. Enhanced or downloaded voices are managed by the operating system, not by a developer server. The Apple Watch companion does not play pronunciation audio.

6. Apple Watch synchronization

If you use the companion app, iPhone and Apple Watch exchange a learning list with WatchConnectivity. That transfer stays between your paired devices. It is not a developer cloud account.

The Watch keeps a local cache so cards can appear when the iPhone is not nearby. You choose the items on iPhone. The Watch home screen uses the short label “Nurses” because the full product name does not fit a watch icon well.

A study-window control exists on iPhone. The current source does not schedule operating-system notifications and does not include a notification SDK. Do not expect timed Watch alerts from this version.

7. Third-party services and SDKs

Runtime libraries in the current app include:

  • Flutter and Dart SDK
  • shared_preferences for local key-value storage
  • audioplayers for bundled sound effects
  • flutter_tts for on-device speech
  • characters for text handling

They run on the device. This app does not use them as analytics. audioplayers can fetch remote audio in general, but this product plays only bundled files. The http package appears only as a transitive dependency and is not used by the app’s own Dart code.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting these pages may cause GitHub to process ordinary web logs under GitHub’s terms. The mobile app does not need this website in order to function.

8. Analytics, advertisements, and tracking

The current build does not include analytics, advertising, attribution, or tracking SDKs. In-app privacy copy states that there is no advertising and no analytics or tracking. There is no App Tracking Transparency prompt, because the app does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

9. Data sharing

The developer does not sell learning data and does not operate a server that receives your progress. WatchConnectivity shares a learning list only with your paired Apple Watch. If you contact support through GitHub Issues, GitHub processes that message on its infrastructure.

Apple or Google may process App Store purchases, device backups, and system speech according to their own policies. Those services are not operated by this app.

10. Data security

Local values use the operating-system app sandbox. Keep a device passcode or biometric lock enabled. No method is perfectly secure. This policy does not claim a security certification.

11. Data retention and deletion

On-device study data remains until you change it in the app or remove the app. Deleting English for Nurses from iPhone, iPad, Android, or Apple Watch removes local learning data on that device, unless the operating system restores it from a backup you already made (for example an iCloud or computer backup of the app container). The developer does not keep a cloud copy to erase on a server.

12. Children’s privacy

The app is an educational product. It does not collect personal information from children for accounts, advertising, or analytics. If you write for support, do not include a child’s name, school, or contact details; describe the product problem only.

13. Policy updates

If a later version adds accounts, cloud sync, in-app purchases that collect account data, analytics, advertising, or networked speech, this policy will be updated and the last-updated date will change.

14. Contact and support

Questions about this policy can be opened as a GitHub Issue on the public legal repository (a GitHub account is required):

github.com/bekirs01/english-for-nurses-legal/issues

No separate support email address is published in the current project files. See also the Support page.
